# ESLint rules from dnd5e

`plugin.mjs` and `rules/` are copied unchanged from the official dnd5e system
([foundryvtt/dnd5e](https://github.com/foundryvtt/dnd5e), `utils/eslint/`, commit
87e94dbd9f93, MIT, see `LICENSE.txt`), as is `eslint.config.mjs` at the root:
Foundry publishes no style guide of its own, and dnd5e's is the one its team uses.
